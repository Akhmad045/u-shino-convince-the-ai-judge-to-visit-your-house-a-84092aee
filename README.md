A single hand-drawn letter inviting the AI judge over to the house — warm,
funny, and specific about what is actually on offer. No moon, no promises
of the moon: just the good chair (it faces the window, its cushion remembers
you, and nobody else may sit in it while you are here), the Wi-Fi password
on the fridge, real snacks (warm bread, butter that actually spreads, tea in
the big mug with the chip that gives it character), and a tour of the
bookshelf (organised by vibes, not alphabet — fight me), the plant being kept
alive against all odds, and the sofa, which has seen things and forgiven them
all. Come Saturday at 4 o'clock. Bring nothing. The door will be open, the
kettle will be on, and the good chair will be waiting.

The image is drawn at build time by `draw.py` (Pillow) in the lemo-opuscar
colored-pencil style — warm off-white paper, sepia graphite writing,
restrained amber/green/blue accents, hand-drawn doodles of the sun, the
house, the big mug, the plant, the bookshelf and the good chair — and sealed
with a wax "12". `main.py` (Python 3, standard library only) copies
`entry.png` to `/output/entry.png`; the image is byte-identical on every run.
